---
title: "Idempotency and ETags"
description: "Safe retries on writes, and safe concurrent changes."
---

Idempotency makes a **retry** safe. `If-Match` makes a **concurrent change** safe.

## Idempotency

| Operation | Key |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /api/v2/external/shipments` | `Idempotency-Key` if sent, otherwise your `reference` |
| `POST /api/v2/external/shipments/{id}/cancel` | `Idempotency-Key` if sent |

Keys are scoped to your merchant account.

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Same key, same request | The original result — `200` rather than `201` on create |
| Same key, different request | `409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` |
| A different key | A new request |

`Idempotency-Key` must be **16–128 characters**; a shorter one is refused with `422 VALIDATION_FAILED`. Derive it from the business intent that must happen at most once — never generate a fresh random key per retry.

## ETags and If-Match

Shipments and your webhook carry a strong numeric ETag, `"<version>"`, quotes included. The same number is the `version` field. A conditional request is required for:

- `PATCH /api/v2/external/shipments/{id}`
- `PATCH /api/v2/external/webhook`
- `DELETE /api/v2/external/webhook`
- `POST /api/v2/external/webhook/secret-rotations`

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `428 PRECONDITION_REQUIRED` | No `If-Match` | Read the resource, then retry with its ETag |
| `412 PRECONDITION_FAILED` | Your ETag is stale | Re-read, decide whether your change still makes sense, then retry |

The `ETag` comes back on reads and on every successful change, so a sequence of changes can chain without an extra `GET`. A shipment's version also advances as it moves through its lifecycle, so read it shortly before you change it.
